I'm a senior researcher at [Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/), where I study what drives AI progress, how fast it's going, and how it impacts the world. 

## Projects
Some things that I've worked on: 
- I [estimated](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.05812) how fast AI algorithms are [improving](https://epochai.substack.com/p/the-least-understood-driver-of-ai)
- I helped develop a [statistical framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.00193) to aggregate AI benchmark scores, which is now used in the [Epoch Capabilities Index](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/eci) and by big AI companies
- I estimated how much you can improve the [energy efficiency of modern computer chips](https://epoch.ai/publications/limits-to-the-energy-efficiency-of-cmos-microprocessors)
- I contributed to the [2025](https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/publication/international-ai-safety-report-2025) and [2026](https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/publication/international-ai-safety-report-2026) International AI Safety Reports, which are some of the largest collaborations on AI safety to date
- I helped write research articles for [Epoch's substack](https://epochai.substack.com/), such as about [US-China AI competition](https://epochai.substack.com/p/keeping-up-with-the-gpts), [measuring AI capabilities](https://epochai.substack.com/p/how-close-is-ai-to-taking-my-job) in the wild, and [energy bottlenecks to AI scaling](https://epochai.substack.com/p/is-almost-everyone-wrong-about-americas), among many other things. 

You can find more of my previous work on [Epoch's website](https://epoch.ai/about/team/anson-ho) and [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=MlaT7lsAAAAJ). I also write quite a bit, mostly on [Substack](https://substack.com/@ansonwhho). You can find my older (and worse) writing [here](/writings/).
